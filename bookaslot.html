HOW IT WORKS

From first brief to confirmed production.

01

The Review

We evaluate your concept, requirements, and timing to determine whether we are the right studio for your project, our current availability, and the initial production scope.

02

The Conversation

A 30-minute video or phone consultation to walk through the creative brief, explore reference directions, discuss specific production needs, and establish budget parameters.

03

The Proposal

You receive a detailed creative proposal outlining shoot structure, shot list, crew requirements, post-production timeline, licensing rights, and an exact production cost estimate.

TYPICAL LEAD TIME: 2–4 WEEKS FOR EDITORIAL · 4–8 WEEKS FOR COMMERCIAL & ADVERTISING

You don’t need a comprehensive brief to get started. We often shape concepts and creative direction collaboratively with clients before locking in production scope.

Perfect. If your creative team has established the visual direction, we step in to execute it with technical precision, bespoke lighting design, and meticulous set execution.

Depending on our current production schedule and studio availability, we occasionally accommodate expedited shoots. Please note urgent timing in the form above and we will see what is possible.
